Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last Updated: May 2025

01 — Overview

Who We Are

Shawn's Garage Door is a locally owned garage door installation, repair, and service company based in Sioux City, Iowa. We operate this website to help customers request quotes, learn about our services, and get in touch with us directly.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We do not sell your data — period.

02 — Information We Collect

What We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact and quote request forms. Here's a breakdown:

Data Type Why We Collect It Required?
Full Name To address you personally and identify your request Required
Phone Number To call you back and schedule your service Required
Email Address To send quote details or follow-up information Required
Street Address To provide an accurate service estimate Optional
Service Description / Message To understand your needs before calling Optional

We do not collect payment information through this website. All payments are handled directly at time of service.

03 — How We Use It

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and schedule or provide garage door services. Specifically:

— To call or email you back regarding your quote request
— To schedule a service appointment at your location
— To follow up after a completed job if needed

We do not use your information for marketing emails, third-party advertising, or any purpose unrelated to your service request.

04 — Sharing

Who We Share With

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. Your information stays with Shawn's Garage Door.

The only exceptions are standard service providers who help us operate (such as our website hosting provider or email platform), and they are contractually prohibited from using your data for anything other than providing us that service.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our business or customers.

05 — Data Retention

How Long We Keep It

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to complete your service request and any reasonable follow-up. If you'd like your information removed from our records, contact us and we will honor that request promptly.

06 — Your Rights

Your Rights

You have the right to:

— Access the personal information we hold about you
— Correct any inaccurate information
— Request deletion of your personal information
— Opt out of any future communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, reach out to us directly using the contact information below.
